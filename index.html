The Five Pillars

Finance · Music · Body · Relationships · Faith

Decide in advance, so in the moment you're following your own rules rather than relying on willpower.

01Floors & Standards

The standard is a normal week. The floor is the minimum on a chaotic week. Hitting the floor counts as a win.

02Never Miss Twice

Missing once is life. Missing twice is the start of a new habit. After a miss, the next session is protected no matter what.

03Built-in White Space
  • Friday evening, Saturday evening and one open weekday evening stay unplanned on purpose.
  • Use them for friends, church community, rest, or catching up a missed session.
  • Midweek church commitments are part of the faith pillar, not extras.
04Rules for Invites
  • Invites on Fri evening, Sat evening or your open evening get an easy yes.
  • On a protected slot, swap the session, don't skip it. It moves elsewhere that week.
  • Pause before accepting: "Let me check my week and come back to you."
  • Offer another time: "Can't do Thursday, how about Saturday?"
05Rules for Energy
  • Every session has a tired version. Quant → review. Music → listen back or arrange.
  • Commit to the first 10 minutes. Still flat after that? Stop, no guilt.
  • Eat around 4–5pm on gym days.
  • Bedtime around 10:30 to support the 6:15 starts.
06Sunday Planning

15 minutes on Sunday evening to check the template against the real week.

  • Who am I seeing this week?
  • Which days are busy, and which sessions need to move?
  • Which days will run at the floor?
07Make Bad Habits Inconvenient
  • Phone charges outside the bedroom.
  • Site blocker on during quant and music sessions.
  • Make the good option the easy default.
